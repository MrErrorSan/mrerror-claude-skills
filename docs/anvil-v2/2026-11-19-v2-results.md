## What we learned

- **The 1.0 rebuild's machinery was the cost**, not the idea of a governor. The original text-only Anvil ran at Superpowers speed with the best accuracy.
- **Dormant by default (2.2) fixed it.** Linear work takes "just do it" with no ceremony, and routing was correct on 21/21 runs. 2.2 is now the judge's top arm, as fast as Superpowers overall, with the joint-best accuracy.
- **The one remaining gap is c5 ("spec first"): 619 s against the original's 319 s.** The time goes into the build phase after the stop (330–430 s), because it still loads the full build and verify skills.

## Next, when there's usage to spend

Saad's direction: **optimise the approach that worked (the original), rather than building new machinery.**
1. Take the original 0.1.0's tier text as the base. Keep from 2.2 only what measurably helped: the dormant hooks, landing the work, the plain report with calls listed, approve-with-edits, and the line-endings and Edit/Write rules.
2. Spec first: after the stop, build the way "just do it" does (no build or verify skill loads). Target: c5 under 350 s.
